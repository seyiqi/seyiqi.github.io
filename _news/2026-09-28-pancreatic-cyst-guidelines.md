Our study <a href="https://www.jacr.org/article/S1546-1440(26)00485-0/fulltext">evaluating pancreatic cyst guidelines for long-term malignancy risk prediction</a> was published in the <em>Journal of the American College of Radiology</em>! Using LLM-extracted radiology features from 13,269 patients, we compared ACR, Kyoto, and European guidelines to inform more individualized surveillance.
